# AI usage policy

Issues and pull requests in this repository may be written with AI assistance. The rules are short.

1. **A human is responsible.** Whatever tool produced the text, a human must review it, be able to
   explain it, and answer follow-up questions about it. "The model wrote that" is not an answer.
2. **Disclose.** Say whether the text was written by a human or with AI assistance, and name the tools
   used. The issue and pull request forms ask for this explicitly.
3. **Verify.** For a bug report, a human must have reproduced the problem. For a pull request, a human
   must understand every change in it. Both forms ask you to copy a confirmation phrase exactly — that
   phrase is a statement of responsibility, not a formality.
4. **Describe a real case, do not generate one.** A feature request should describe a concrete workflow
   that fell short, not a list of plausible ideas.
5. **No secrets and no personal data.** Keys, tokens, credentials, work documents and personal notes do
   not belong in an issue or a pull request. Redact them; if the report is impossible to explain
   without them, describe their shape rather than their value.
6. **A small honest report beats a long confident one.** "I do not know why this happens; here is what
   I ran and what I saw" is more useful than a plausible guess — and it is what we can act on.

Reports that ignore these rules may be closed without an answer. Nothing here restricts what you do
with your own copy of the code: the rules apply to what you send to this repository.
